World-Class Services

Fine Dining

Three Michelin-starred restaurant helmed by Chef Jean-Pierre Moreau. Farm-to-table cuisine with Egyptian and French influences, paired with our cellar of over 3,000 wines.

Infinity Pool

A heated rooftop infinity pool with panoramic views of the Nile. Open 24 hours for our guests, with private cabanas and expert poolside service.

Luxury Spa

A sanctuary of 2,400 m² featuring 22 treatment rooms, a hammam, and therapies drawing from ancient Egyptian healing traditions and modern wellness science.

Concierge & Transport

24/7 personal concierge service. Complimentary Rolls-Royce airport transfers, private yacht charters, and helicopter excursions over the pyramids available upon request.

Events & Weddings

Six bespoke event spaces accommodating from 10 to 1,000 guests. Our event architects curate every detail — from intimate soirées to grand celebrations.

Fitness & Wellness

A state-of-the-art fitness centre with private training sessions, yoga studios, and an Olympic-length lap pool. Your wellness journey, personalized.
